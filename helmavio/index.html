Helmavio

On the Microsoft Store

Your own AI team.
Built your way.

Helmavio is not one more chatbot. It is a workspace where you assemble your own team of AI agents and give it real work. Describe a goal in plain words — Helmavio plans it, hands the steps to the right agents, runs them and proves the result. What your team can do is limited only by the agents and models you connect.

See what it can do

How it works

From a sentence to verified work.

  1. 1

    Say what you need

    Write the goal the way you would explain it to a colleague. No prompts to engineer, no agents to pick by hand.

  2. 2

    Review the plan

    The Orchestrator drafts a mission with a clear Definition of Done, the team, tools, approvals and risk — and shows it to you before anything runs.

  3. 3

    Get proven results

    Agents do the work. An independent Reviewer checks it against every criterion and keeps a proof receipt you can replay step by step.

The real potential

Connect any agent. Grow any capability.

Helmavio is a platform: every agent and model you add becomes a new skill of your team. Start with the built-in roles and expand as far as your ideas go.

◆

Built-in roles

Orchestrator plans, Researcher / Analyst studies and structures information, Operator does the hands-on work, Reviewer verifies it. They work out of the box.

◇

Your own agents

Create an agent from a description, import an agent card, or bring in agents from plugins — for example, Claude Code agents you already use.

△

Ready-made teams

Start from team templates: indie game, mobile app release, research, planning and security review — then adjust them to your project.

○

Tools via MCP

Plug in MCP servers to give agents new tools — per project and per tool, with your approval for anything risky.

◈

Remote agents via A2A

Connect agents that live elsewhere through the open A2A protocol, plus GitHub and Telegram connections.

◎

Any model you choose

Local models through Ollama — free and offline; Claude Code with your Claude account; or any OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible API with your own key.

Visual control

See everything. Approve what matters.

Plan first

Nothing runs until you have seen the plan.

Every mission starts as a contract: the goal, what “done” means, the team, tools, approvals and risk. Edit it, simulate it or run it.

  • Definition of Done checked automatically
  • Plain-language goal, no prompt engineering

Simulation

Know the impact before it happens.

A simulation predicts which files, tools and network access a mission will touch, its cost class, its risk and the approvals it will need.

  • Files, tools, network and cost at a glance
  • Certainty labels, not guesses

Approvals

Agents work in a sandbox. You hold the keys.

Anything outside the sandbox waits in “Needs attention” with the exact action shown. A live map shows which agent is doing what, and every mission can be replayed later.

  • Live map of your agents, with replay
  • Monitoring, history and proof receipts
  • Keeps working in the background and recovers after crashes

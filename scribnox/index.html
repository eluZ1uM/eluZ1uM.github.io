Scribnox

On the Microsoft Store

From manuscript
to finished book.

Scribnox is a calm writing studio for Windows. Bring your manuscript — typed, pasted or imported — and watch it become a real book: chapters, a cover, a title page and a printed-style spread. When it is ready, Scribnox packages it for the stores readers actually use.

How it works

The path

Three steps to a book you can publish.

  1. 1

    Bring the text

    Write in Scribnox, paste a solid block of text or import TXT, Markdown, DOCX or PDF. Everything is saved automatically.

  2. 2

    Shape it into a book

    Chapters are detected for you. Add a cover, title page and book details, and read it as a real two-page spread.

  3. 3

    Publish

    Export EPUB, PDF, DOCX and FB2 and prepare a ready package for Amazon KDP, Apple Books, Litres and other stores.

A real book, not a document

See your book the way readers will.

Scribnox lays your manuscript out page by page as a printed spread, so structure, rhythm and length are easy to judge while you write.

  • Prose and verse layouts, type size and spacing
  • Light and dark themes for long writing sessions
  • Russian and English — interface and manuscripts

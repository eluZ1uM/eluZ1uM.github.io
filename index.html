What we make

Three products. One standard.

Scribnox

On the Microsoft Store

From manuscript to finished book.

A quiet writing studio for Windows. Paste or import your manuscript and Scribnox lays it out as a real book — chapters, cover, title page — ready for readers and stores.

  • Write, edit and see your book as a printed spread
  • EPUB, PDF, DOCX and FB2 for stores like Amazon KDP, Apple Books and Litres (Pro)
  • Optional AI with your own key — paragraphs and chapters placed for you, your words never rewritten
More about Scribnox

Helmavio

On the Microsoft Store

Your AI agents, under your control.

An AI agent workspace for Windows. Describe what you need in plain words; Helmavio drafts a plan with clear completion criteria, picks the agents, runs the steps and checks the result.

  • Plans with a Definition of Done, checked automatically
  • Agents work in a sandbox — anything outside asks for your approval
  • Monitoring, history and evidence for every mission
More about Helmavio

Pushup Battle

In development

Push-up duels, live.

A 1v1 online fitness game. Two players, two cameras, one timer — the app counts every rep and the better set wins.

  • Live video of your opponent during the battle
  • Reps counted on your device by pose detection — no cloud analysis
  • iPhone first, then Android

About the studio
